§ 1 — Research

My research sits across machine learning, computer vision, and natural language processing — three areas that are increasingly difficult, and increasingly unproductive, to treat as separate. I am interested in how models represent and reason about the world, both visually and linguistically, and in where those representations break down.

I work with two faculty members at IIIT Delhi across three active projects. The applied mathematics component of my degree shapes how I approach problems: I tend to start with the formalism before the implementation, which is slower but has so far been worth it.

I am actively looking for research internships for Semester VIII (early 2026). If you are working on problems in ML, CV, or NLP and are open to undergraduate collaborators, I would be glad to hear from you.

§ 4 — Broader Interests

Outside my current projects, I am drawn to questions about model robustness and generalisation — why systems that perform well in one setting fail quietly in another. I am also interested in the mathematical foundations that underpin modern deep learning, particularly the geometry of representation spaces and what it means for two representations to be equivalent.

Longer term, I am interested in pursuing graduate study — likely a PhD — in ML or a closely adjacent field. I am in no hurry to narrow down further than that.
